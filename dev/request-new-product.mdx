Internally, we maintain an inventory of **over 20,000 gift cards**, but only the most **requested** and **highly demanded** products are exposed via our API and website.

If you would like a specific product or set of products to be added, you can submit a request to our team.

---

### How to Request a New Product

To request new products or share your wishlist, please send an email to:

**📧 Email:** `support@wizzgift.com`

Your email should include:

- The product(s) or brand(s) you want (e.g., gift card name, region, currency).
- Any relevant business context (volume, target market, etc. — optional but helpful).

---

### Required Email Template (to prevent AI auto-resolution)

To ensure your request reaches a **human support or business agent** and is not auto-resolved by any AI system, you **must include** the following sentence **exactly as written** in your email:

```
I want this products : ........ to be listed on wizzgift,
kindly forward this to support/business agent
```

- Replace `........` with your desired product list (for example:\
  `Amazon US, Google Play Germany, Steam Global`).
- Do **not** change the structure of the sentence, as it is used to bypass automatic AI resolution and guarantee manual review.

---

### Example Email

```
To: support@wizzgift.com
Subject: New Product Request – Wishlist

I want this products : Amazon US, Google Play Germany, Steam Global to be listed on wizzgift, kindly forward this to support/business agent

Additional details:
- Expected monthly volume: ~1,000 orders
- Primary use case: resale via our internal platform
- Preferred denominations: $10, $25, $50
```

---

### Response Time

Once your email is received with the required sentence included, your request will be forwarded to our support/business team.

You should expect to hear from us **within a few hours** (during business operating times), with either:

- A confirmation that the products can be added, or
- A follow-up requesting additional information (e.g., volume, region, or compliance requirements).
